### Develop your career in data science


The Sydney Informatics Hub is looking for coders, statisticians and graduates from quantitative/computational research degrees to join our diverse team, which assists researchers in using data to solve their diverse research questions.

The roles are well suited for candidates transitioning to or from academic research work.

We will be regularly advertising data science / research engineer roles with focusses on software engineering, statistics, business and health.
