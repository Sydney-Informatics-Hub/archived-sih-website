Congrats to David Kohn from SIH and the rest of the #Circadian rhythm team for sharing victory at the [ANZICS](http://www.anzics.com.au/www.anzics.com.au/index.html) (Australian and New Zealand Intensive Care Society) [Datathon](http://datathon.anzics.com.au/) last month!

David and his teammates explored the presence of circadian rhythm oscillations in data recorded from patients in ICU. Whether these rhythms are present and what their presentation might reveal are of keen interest to ICU physicians.
