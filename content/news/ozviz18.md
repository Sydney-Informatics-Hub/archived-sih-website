SIH made an appearence at the [OzViz 2018](https://sites.google.com/site/ozvizworkshop/ozviz-2018) workshop where we learned about the latest and greatest in visualisation practice. The workshop took place within the facilities at the University of New South Wales' Expanded Perception and Visualisation Interaction Centre, or [EPICentre](https://artdesign.unsw.edu.au/research/epicentre-expanded-perception-interaction-centre), which houses a range of visualisation technologies (think the highest resolotuion 340 degree, 3 metre tall HD screen set-up) that offer novel ways of displaying and exploring data.

Other immersive technologies were on display from the [Wonderama Lab](http://eresearch.uws.edu.au/blog/2014/03/24/967/) at the University of Western Sydney, the [Data Arena](https://www.uts.edu.au/partners-and-community/data-arena/overview) at the University of Technology Sydney, the [Hive](https://humanities.curtin.edu.au/research/centres-institutes-groups/hive/) at Curtin University, and more.

Moving forward we are excited to continue working with researchers to support their data visualisation needs. Join us at our [Hacky Hour](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/hackyhour/) or drop us an [email](mailto:sih.info@sydney.edu.au) to accelerate your research of tomorrow, today!
