The Sydney Informatics Hub was pleased to host trainers from the [Pawsey Supercomputing Centre](https://www.pawsey.org.au/) in W.A. once again this past week, following on the heels of their last visit in September 2017.

Pawsey's expert trainers covered a range of topics focussed on getting researchers familiar with their supercomputing [resources](https://www.pawsey.org.au/our-systems/). A highight was the demonstration of the [Nimbus research cloud](https://www.pawsey.org.au/our-services/data/cloud-services/) -- a cloud computing platform available to _any_ Australian researcher, running a neat and friendly OpenStack portal. Check it out!

If you missed out on the course, you can still find some of their training materials [here](https://support.pawsey.org.au/documentation/display/US/Training+Material), as well as their [_Using Nimbus_](https://pawseysupercomputing.github.io/using-nimbus/) tutorial.

Of course, Pawsey's main platform, the petaflop Magnus supercomputer, is also accessible to University researchers through several access schemes including the [NCMAS](https://ncmas.nci.org.au/2018/) national grant scheme.

As always, if you have questions about accessing supercomputing resources, either on the University of Sydney's own recently-upgraded [Artemis](/services/artemis) cluster or other national resources like those at NCI or Pawsey, give us a howl at [sih.training@sydney.edu.au](mailto:sih.training@sydney.edu.au).
