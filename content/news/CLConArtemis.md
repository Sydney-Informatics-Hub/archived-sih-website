The Sydney Informatics Hub is pleased to announce that the [CLC Genomics Workbench](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/services/bioinformatics/) has been integrated with the University's High Performance Computer Artemis! This will enable researchers to perform their genomics, transcriptomics, epigenomics and metagenomics analysis with even higher throughput on a user friendly platform (no command line knowledge necessary!).

We are also releasing an [Introduction to CLC Genomics on Artemis](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/#clc) course. This course is suitable for current users as well as anyone else who is interested in purchasing a subscription to the CLC Genomics Workbench.  

We also have other bioinformatics training courses:

* Introduction to DNA sequence analysis on Galaxy
* Introduction to RNA sequence analysis on Galaxy
* Intermediate to DNA sequence analysis on Artemis (coming soon)
* Introduction to RNA sequence analysis on Artemis (coming soon)

For dates and to register into a course please see our [training schedule](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/). 

As always, if you have questions about accessing facilities provided or supported by the Sydney Informatics Hub, including Ingenuity Pathway Analysis, or the University of Sydney's own recently-upgraded [Artemis](/services/artemis) cluster, give us a howl at [sih.training@sydney.edu.au](mailto:sih.training@sydney.edu.au).
