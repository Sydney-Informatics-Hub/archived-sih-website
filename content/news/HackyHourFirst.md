Teams from SIH, ICT, The Library have banded together and ran the first Hacky Hour at Sydney University.

We had over 30 researchers pop in from all different disciplines getting help with things ranging from "how do I make my Python code print to a csv file?" to "What does this error mean?".

If success is rated by the inverse of how many chocolates are left at the end of a session, then it was a very successful meet up.
The next Hacky Hour will be hosted at Westmead on the 15th of March 2018. See https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/hackyhour/ for all the details.
