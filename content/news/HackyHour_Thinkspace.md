SIH's [Sydney Hacky Hour](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/hackyhour/) achieved NextLevel™ last Wednesday with our first time at the Library's incredible [Thinkspace](https://library.sydney.edu.au/libraries/thinkspace.html) on Jane Foss Russel Plaza. Not only did we have the usual good company, serious research collaboration and quality snacks (mini cupcakes!), but the Library's Pear Learning Advisors, as well as Sonya Corcoran from [USYD TechLab](https://www.facebook.com/usydtechlab/) were on hand to demonstrate Thinkspace's amazing tech, including 3D printers, doodlers and etchers.

However, the biggest hit was probably the Oculus VR headsets, which were, in this humble blogger's opinion, pretty incredible. Not the nauseating 3D anaglyph glasses you watched Avatar in, these current generation of headsets, sensors and controllers need to be experienced to be believed. No need to wait for next Hacky Hour, the Thinkspace is open Mon-Fri!
