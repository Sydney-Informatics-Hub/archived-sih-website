## Artemis: The University of Sydney High-Performance Supercomputer

The Artemis Phase 3 expansion continues to make news headlines around the world with its cutting edge technology. The cluster is a 7636-core InfiniBand cluster powered by DELL EMC PowerEdge's, with an additional 108 NVIDIA V100 GPUs. 

The expansion of the University of Sydney's HPC rolled out in February 2018, and a few months later the investment of $2.3 Million is showing its results. Work performed with Artemis varies across the University and includes understanding the [Earth's dynamical systems](https://github.com/badlands-model), determining the [intergalactic distances and speed limits](http://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4357/aa7ed2/pdf), to investigating how [plastic particles move around](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2017.653)
