Congratulations to all Australian Research Council (ARC) grantees in the November round!

University of Sydney researchers have been awarded a further 71 funding grants for 2018 -- bringing the University's total ARC awards this year to over $52 million.

We at the Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) are especially proud that 31 Artemis HPC Chief Investigators were amongst these new recipients, representing $13 million worth of research funding.

Find out how SIH can help accelerate and scale up your research -- check out our [Services](/services), or put in a [help request](https://sydney.edu.au/data-science/assistance).

Once again, congrats to all the grantees!
