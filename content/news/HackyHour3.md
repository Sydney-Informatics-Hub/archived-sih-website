Sydney Hacky Hour just turned 3! And we couldn't have asked for a better session than was co-hosted by the [ICT Techlab](https://twitter.com/usydtechlab?lang=en). As well as the usual research questions and expertise sharing, HH@Techlab allowed us to connect with more University professionals and staff members grappling with their own research and data wrangling challenges.
