The Sydney Informatics Hub, in collaboration with the Brain and Mind Centre and Sydney Uni Techlab, is running a _[Coding Challenge](https://sydney.edu.au/about-us/innovation-week/supercomputing-code-challenge.html)_ as part of Sydney University's [Innovation Week](https://sydney.edu.au/about-us/innovation-week.html) (30 July – 3 August).

The Challenge (which will be managed via our [Kaggle competition](https://www.kaggle.com/c/logos-speechrecog)) will require contestants to develop a solution for automatically scoring phone-recorded responses to a memory test -- as part of the [Valenzuela lab](https://sydney.edu.au/brain-mind/our-research/healthy-ageing-and-neurodegeneration/regenerative-neuroscience-group.html)'s LOGOS dementia diagnosis project.

Speech recognition in this setting is actually more difficult than transcribing natural speech, as the words are random and hence there is no _semantic context_ to help algorithms guess what was just said. In fact, Google's transcription models perform only about 65% accurately on this task.

Think you can do better? Register for the Challenge using the form below!
