Dr. Gordon McDonald presented at the Health Data Analytics conference in Brisbane about the use of Bayesian statistics in health care data. The project was a collaboration between the Dr. Gordon McDonald and Dr. Aldo Saavedra at Sydney Informatics Hub at the University of Sydney, Dr. Louise Sealy and Prof. Karen Zwi at the University of New South Wales, and Lisa Crawford and Prof. Hasantha Gunasekera the Sydney Childrens Hospital Network. We analyzed historical hospital admission records to look at discharge against medical advice in the Sydney Children's Hospital Network using bayesian techniques.
