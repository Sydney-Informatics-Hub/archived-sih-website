The [Intersect HPC Resource Allocation Scheme](
https://intersect.org.au/help/solutions/time-intersect-org-au/supertime/2020-intersect-hpc-resource-allocation-open/) is now open and closes Friday 18th October, 2019.  

This scheme enables researchers to access the National Computational Infrastructure facility (NCI)'s Gadi High Performance Computing Cluster, at no cost to researchers.  
It is a great opportunity to position your project for the competitive [National Computational Merit Allocation Scheme (NCMAS)](http://ncmas.nci.org.au/).   

Eligibility criteria and application requirements are strict. Competitive applications present preliminary results generated on NCI and demonstrate the scalability of their code across multiple nodes.  

The Sydney Informatics Hub (SIH) are providing assistance to University of Sydney researchers with preparing their applications, preliminary results, and code scalability and benchmarking on NCI. SIH wil also help transition analysis and data processing workflows from Artemis, HPC to NCI. We have expertise to assist with modelling, simulation, machine learning and genomics projects.

SIH will be hosting an information session to present an overview of what these requirements are and how SIH can assist researchers in meeting them to promote a successful outcome.  

Researchers who have applied for NCMAS 2020 (or wish to apply for NCMAS 2021), or whose computational needs for 2020 will exceed that provided by Artemis HPC are encouraged to attend. 

For further information about the Intersect HPC Resource Allocation Scheme or how SIH can assist with your application please contact [sih.info@sydney.edu.au](mailto:sih.info@sydney.edu.au)
