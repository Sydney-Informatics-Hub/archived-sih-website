The Research Data Consulting team provides expertise, training and support on effective management of research data.

Utilising appropriate Research Data Management (RDM) processes and platforms enables researchers to preserve the integrity and continuity of their research. It also allows researchers to align their research practice with expectations outlined in University and National policy

For more information please contact:

* Data Management consultations and tailored training - <digital.research@sydney.edu.au>
* Policy Consulting and Queries - <adele.haythornthwaite@sydney.edu.au>

## Researcher Dashboard (DashR)

The [Researcher Dashboard (DashR)](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/rdm/dashr/) is a centralised location for researchers to register details about their research projects’ data practices and to request/manage associated data services.

DashR can be used to:

* Enter and maintain your Research Data Management Planning (RDMP) details
* Request data storage on the [Research Data Store Classic (RDS)](https://sydneyuni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RC/pages/229146744/Classic+RDS) or [Research Computing Optimised Storage (RCOS)](https://sydneyuni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RC/pages/229015798/Research+Computing+Optimised+Storage+RCOS)
* Request high performance computing (HPC) services on [Artemis](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/services/artemis/)
* Control and update user access to the above services

## Training Courses

We currently offer three training courses open to any University of Sydney staff, students and affiliates:

* [Introduction to REDCap](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/#introred)
* [Advanced REDCap](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/#advred)
* [eNotebooks and other RDM tools](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/#enotes)

We also have a series of short [online lessons](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/rdm/hdr-modules/) which provide an overview of research data management concepts at the University. While it is aimed at HDR students, it is a useful resource for anyone wanting to know more about research data management. 

For details on upcoming sessions and to register, visit [Sydney Informatics Hub Training](https://informatics.sydney.edu.au/training/).
