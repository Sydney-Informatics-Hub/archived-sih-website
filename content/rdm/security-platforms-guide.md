This guidance categorises the systems according to the level of [data classification] (https://sydneyuni.service-now.com/sm?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=26381b9adbc973485beaf9b7f496197c) that is appropriate to be stored. Most systems require you to use [file encryption] (https://sydneyuni.service-now.com/sm?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=02addf16db4173c8c8a5773c34961900) when working with Highly Confidential data. Software such as [Cryptomator](https://sydneyuni.service-now.com/sm?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=02addf16db4173c8c8a5773c34961900) can be used to encrypt your files on data stores. [Multi-Factor Authentication] (https://sydneyuni.service-now.com/sm?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=63a8e085db06fb445beaf9b7f496195c)  is strongly recommended, especially when handling Highly Confidential Data.
