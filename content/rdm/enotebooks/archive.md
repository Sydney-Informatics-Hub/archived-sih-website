Once your research project is complete you will need to keep your eNotebook as part of your research data. There are a variety of options and considerations depending on your particular situation.

For practical guidance on how to archive data from an eNotebook please see the resource sheet on [archiving completed eNotebooks](https://library.sydney.edu.au/research/data-management/archiving-data.html).

## How long do I need to keep my eNotebook?

eNotebooks are considered to be research data under the University's Research Data Management Policy and need to be retained for a minimum of 5 years, or possibly longer depending on the nature of the research. For further information about retention periods see the [Research Code of Conduct, Section 9 (6)](http://sydney.edu.au/policies/showdoc.aspx?recnum=PDOC2013/321).
