LabArchives is a web-based platform and can be accessed from any web browser on any mobile device. Just go to the [Login page] (https://aushib.labarchives.com/select_institution) directly in your prefered mobile device browser.  

A LabArchives mobile App is also available on both Android and iOS systems, with set up instructions below.


### Android
1. First, download the [LabArchives Android app] (https://play.google.com/store/search?q=labarchives&c=apps&hl=en) from the Play Store.
2. Once downloaded, open the app and you will be directed to settings
3. Select your server location: "Australia and New Zealand" 
4. You can also change the setting for: 
5. The other settings allows you to activate:
   * Autologin (**“On”** - will prevent you from needing to repeat this process. NOT recommended if you share a device)
    * The ability to use "Google Docs for PDF files" (NOT recommended to manage your research data - default **"OFF"**)
    * Receive Notifications
    * Include students notebooks (for instructors) in your list of notebooks.
6. Login - choose the **"Institutional Login"**
7. Select "University of Sydney"
8. Enter your **Unikey** and **password**

For more information about using your eNotebook in your mobile device visit the [LAbarchive mobile App](http://labarchives.kayako.com/Knowledgebase/List/Index/226) Knowledgebase.  

### iOS 
1. First, download the [LabArchives iOS app] (https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/labarchives/id533469293?mt=8) from the Apple iTunes store.
2. Once downloaded, open the app and you will be directed to settings
3. Set USER LOCATION (API URL) to: "Australia and New Zealand" 
4. You can also change the setting for: 
5. The other settings allows you to activate: 
    * Autologin (**“On”** - will prevent you from needing to repeat this process. NOT recommended if you share a device)
    * Custom server (allows you to add the URL to a custom server - choose **"OFF"**)
    * Receive Notifications
    * Include students notebooks (for instructors) in your list of notebooks. 
6. Login - choose the **"Login through your institution"**
7. Select "University of Sydney"
8. Enter your **Unikey** and **password**

For more information about using your eNotebook in your mobile device visit the [LAbarchive mobile App](http://labarchives.kayako.com/Knowledgebase/List/Index/266/72-ios-mobile-app) Knowledgebase.
